Past Festivals

November 16, 2026 | London, England

Being Adolph Gasser was selected as a Finalist for the International Film Gala / London Film Festival, placing it among the top 10% of all submissions. With 1,692 submissions received from 63 countries, the final selection was exceptionally competitive.

November 6, 2026 | Austin, Texas

Being Adolph Gasser was selected as a Quarter-Finalist for The VSPChannel / AVAFEST Veteran Military Film Festival in Austin, TX. The festival is affiliated with the Veteran's Suicide Prevention Channel, founded in 2015 by a Vietnam combat veteran to reduce veteran suicide and promote mental health healing.

During WWII, Adolph Gasser enlisted despite being about 10 years past draft age and served with the 509th Composite Group on Tinian Island, helping produce the iconic images of a new age of warfare.

October 17, 2026 | Jackson, Tennessee

Being Adolph Gasser was selected as a Finalist in the American History Feature category at the Jackson Doc Fest in Tennessee. So proud of the team and all their efforts to make this documentary as impactful as it is.

February 26–28, 2026 | St. George, Utah

Screening: Thursday, February 26 | 12:15 PM (MST) | Theater 4

Being Adolph Gasser has been selected by the DOCUTAH International Film Festival, Feb. 26–28, 2026, in St. George, Utah. This was the first film festival I submitted it to and one of the best I have attended in the past. Come see the film in a great location with wonderful programmers.
